Privacy Policy — Burrow (버로우)

Last updated: 2026-11-01
Developer: Songgun Lee
Contact: yagurootajum@gmail.com
App: Burrow (com.sgjlee.burrow)

Summary

Burrow collects no data. It has no account, no servers, no analytics, no ads, no third-party SDKs and no AI services. It makes no network requests.

What Burrow accesses, and why

  • Your photo library (with your permission): to copy your photos and videos to an external SSD, USB drive or folder that you choose; to find exact duplicate photos and extra burst frames; and, only after you confirm in iOS's own dialog, to delete photos you chose to remove. All of this happens on your iPhone.
  • The drive or folder you pick: Burrow writes copies only into a folder named Burrow there. It never changes or deletes any other file on that drive.
  • Settings kept on your iPhone: which drive you set up, a record of which files were copied and checked, and which guide steps you ticked. They stay in the app's storage on your phone and are deleted when you delete the app.

What leaves your iPhone

Nothing, except the photo and video copies written to the drive or folder you choose. If you choose an iCloud Drive folder, Apple stores those copies under Apple's own privacy policy.

Children

Burrow collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes and the new policy is posted on this page.
