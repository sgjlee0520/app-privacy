yo, u up? — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-16
Developer: Songgun Lee
Contact: yagurootajum@gmail.com
App: yo, u up? for iPhone, shown on the Home Screen as “U up?” (com.sgjlee.uup)

Summary

U up? is a free alarm with no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no account. It has no server of its own and never sells or shares data. Everything it keeps stays on your iPhone. The only thing that leaves your phone is your location, sent to Apple Weather to get the forecast for the morning screen.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Your alarm settings: time, repeat days, mission, snooze length, alarm sound and °F/°C.
  • Sounds you record with the microphone to use as your alarm. They're saved in the app's own storage and never uploaded.
  • A small diagnostic log of alarm events (for example “alarm scheduled”, “mission started”), kept in the app's storage so problems can be traced. It is never sent anywhere.
  • All of this is deleted when you delete the app.

Camera

Camera missions (find a color, arms up, wear a hat) look at the live camera picture on your iPhone to check that you did the mission, using Apple's on-device Vision framework. No photo or video is saved or sent anywhere. If you don't allow the camera, camera missions turn into math problems instead.

Location and weather

After you finish the mission, U up? shows the weather where you are. For this it reads your location on your iPhone and sends it to Apple's WeatherKit service to get the forecast. The developer never receives or stores your location. Apple handles the request under its own privacy policy. If you don't allow location, the app works the same and just skips the weather.

Alarms

Alarms are scheduled with Apple's AlarmKit, on your iPhone.

Children

U up? does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes and the new policy is posted on this page.

Support

  • Set an alarm: turn on Alarm, pick the time, and tap the day circles for the days it repeats. With no days picked, it rings once and then switches off.
  • Stopping it: the alarm only stops once you finish your mission. You get 5 snoozes per alarm; after that, there's no way out but the mission. Pressing the side or volume buttons, or closing the app, doesn't stop it. It rings again within a few seconds.
  • Missions: math (3 problems, the default), find a color (red, yellow, green or blue), hold both arms up for 20 seconds, or wear a hat and show your face. Surprise me picks one each time. Use Try this mission (no alarm) to practice first.
  • 45 seconds: while you do the mission, the alarm is quiet. If you haven't finished in 45 seconds, it rings again and you get a fresh try.
  • Your own alarm sound: tap Record a new sound… and record up to 15 seconds (or hand the phone to a friend). It gets louder over time, like the built-in beeps.
  • Morning weather: after the mission you see the weather now, and the low, high and chance of rain from your alarm time to 14 hours later. Pick °F or °C in settings.
  • Test it: Test: ring in 1 minute rings once without changing your alarm. Lock your phone after tapping it.
