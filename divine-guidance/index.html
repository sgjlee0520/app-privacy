Divine Guidance — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-15
Developer: Songgun Lee
Contact: yagurootajum@gmail.com
App: Divine Guidance for iPhone (com.slee.divineguidance)

Summary

Divine Guidance has no account, no ads, and no tracking. It does not sell data. A journal you choose to save stays on your iPhone. When you ask for a verse or a reflection, the words you type are sent to the app's server and then to DeepSeek so it can write the reply.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Journal entries you save: the concern you typed and the reply.
  • Those entries stay in the app's storage on this iPhone. They are removed when you delete an entry, clear the journal, or delete the app.

What is sent

When you choose Verse or Homily, the concern you typed is sent to the app server and then to DeepSeek (api.deepseek.com) to generate the reply. That text is used only to produce the response. It is not used for advertising or tracking, and it is not linked to your identity.

Please do not type medical records, government identifiers, passwords, or financial account details.

What this app is not

Divine Guidance is an AI-generated spiritual reflection tool. It is not a priest, therapist, doctor, lawyer, or emergency service. If you are in immediate danger, call 911. In the United States or Canada, call or text 988 for crisis support. Elsewhere, contact your local emergency service.

Children

Divine Guidance is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes and the new policy is posted on this page.

Support

  • Type what is worrying you, then choose Verse or Homily.
  • Save keeps that reply in the journal on this iPhone. Share sends the text through the iOS share sheet.
  • The reply is generated. It can be incomplete or mistaken. Use it for reflection, not as the only basis for an important decision.
