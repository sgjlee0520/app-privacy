Tomatime — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-12
Developer: Songgun Lee
Contact: yagurootajum@gmail.com
App: Tomatime for Mac, iPhone and Apple Watch (com.sgjlee.tomatime)

Summary

Tomatime has no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and never sells or shares data. Used on its own, it keeps everything on your device (and, if you use iCloud, on your other Macs). Only if you choose to sign in with Apple (to sync your Mac, iPhone and Apple Watch, or to use Groups with friends) does it store a few things online, as described below.

What stays on your device

  • Your settings: the tomato size you picked, auto-join choices for your groups, and Open at Login (macOS keeps that one; turn it off in the menu or in System Settings → General → Login Items).
  • Your tomato minutes: how many minutes you focused each day, for the menu and the Tomato Calendar.
  • On iPhone and Apple Watch: the current tomato, the tomato size you picked, your last break length and how the countdown looks (numbers or red clock).
  • All of this is in the app's own storage and is deleted when you delete the app.

Your other Macs (iCloud)

If you're signed in to iCloud, each Mac saves its daily tomato minutes in your own iCloud storage, so the totals add up across your Macs. Apple stores this under your Apple Account; the developer can't see it. You can turn it off by turning off iCloud for the app in System Settings → your name → iCloud.

Syncing your Mac, iPhone and Apple Watch (optional)

If you sign in with Apple in Tomatime on your iPhone or Mac, your devices follow one shared timer: start, pause or stop a tomato on one and the others follow. For this, the following is stored with Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore), Tomatime's online service, under your account:

  • an account identifier from Sign in with Apple (Tomatime asks Apple for no name and no email);
  • the current timer: tomato or break, its size and length, whether it runs, when it ends or how much is left, and which of your devices changed it last (a random ID Tomatime makes for each device, not a hardware ID);
  • for each iPhone and Apple Watch: push tokens from Apple, so Tomatime's server can update your lock screen countdown and your watch through the Apple Push Notification service. Push messages carry only the timer.

Your Apple Watch talks to Tomatime's server only through your iPhone. Only you can read your timer and device entries. Signing out stops syncing.

Focus ("Dumb Mode"): on the Mac, an optional helper script you install yourself runs your own Shortcuts to switch a Focus; it runs on your Mac and sends nothing anywhere.

Groups (optional, version 1.2 and later)

Groups only start when you choose Groups → Sign in with Apple in the menu. Tomatime asks Apple for no name and no email. Then the following is stored with Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore), which Tomatime uses as its online service:

  • an account identifier from Sign in with Apple;
  • the display name you type;
  • the groups you're in, their names and invite codes;
  • your live status: focusing (tomato size and when it ends), on a break, or idle;
  • your daily tomato minutes, only from the day you joined each group, listed under a random ID that Tomatime makes for each of your Macs (not a hardware ID).

Who sees it: only the people in your groups, and only for the groups you share with them. The developer can technically access the stored data to run the service, and doesn't look at it or use it for anything else. It is not used for advertising or tracking.

Leaving and deleting: leaving a group deletes your status and minutes in that group (the last person to leave deletes the group). Groups → Delete My Groups Account… leaves all your groups, deletes your display name and account, and revokes Tomatime's Sign in with Apple access. Your tomato minutes on your Mac stay. On iPhone, Account → Delete Account… does the same and also deletes your synced timer and device push tokens. You can also ask for deletion by email.

Children

Tomatime does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Syncing and Groups are optional and require Sign in with Apple.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes and the new policy is posted on this page.

Support

  • Tomatime lives in the menu bar (top right of the screen); it has no Dock icon or window. Click the tomato and countdown to open its menu.
  • Pick a tomato: green 20 min, red 25 min, golden 45 min, diamond 60 min. Then click Start.
  • When a session ends, a red screen covers your displays until you choose a break, one more minute, or End session (Esc). Focus sessions never run longer than 60 minutes.
  • Custom tomato… lets you type any length from 1 to 60 minutes. Tomato Calendar… shows your minutes per day.
  • Groups: Groups → New Group… gives you an invite code to send; friends choose Groups → Join with Code…. Start a group tomato from the group's line in the menu; everyone in it gets a 10-second countdown. Pause takes only you out.
  • If you use Reduce Motion (System Settings → Accessibility → Display), the red screen stays steady instead of pulsing.
  • iPhone: pick a tomato and tap Start. The countdown also shows on the Lock Screen. When a tomato ends, pick a 5, 10 or 15 minute break, or Custom (drag around the circle). Choose Numbers or Red clock at the bottom. Sign in with Apple (the same Apple Account as on your Mac) to sync with your Mac and Apple Watch.
  • Apple Watch: pick a tomato from the list. While it runs, swipe between the red clock, the buttons and the time; tap the clock or the time to pause or resume, and ↺ to start over. When a tomato ends, pick a break or turn the Digital Crown for a custom one. Add the Tomatime complication to your watch face to see the countdown with the screen dimmed.
